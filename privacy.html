ME — privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What ME is

ME is a personal iPhone app. There is no ME server and no ME account system: the developer does not receive, store or see any of your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter (workouts, tasks, recipes, receipts, inventory, money movements, photos) is stored on your device. If you continue as a guest, nothing leaves the device.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, ME receives your name, email address and profile picture, only to show who is signed in and to keep one profile per account on your device.

ME also asks for access to its own hidden application folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. This folder holds the backup copy of your ME data, so you can restore it on a new device. You can delete that copy, and revoke ME's access, at any time from the app or from your Google Account settings.

ME's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the sign-in and backup features described here; it is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or read by humans.

Optional AI features

If you enter your own API key for an AI service, the content you ask ME to analyse (for example a photo of a receipt) is sent directly from your device to that service, under its own terms. The key stays on your device.
